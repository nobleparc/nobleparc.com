  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p><em>Last updated: August 2026</em></p>

  <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
  <p>When you purchase from Nobleparc, we collect:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Contact information:</strong> Name, email address, shipping address, phone number (provided during checkout via Stripe).</li>
    <li><strong>Order information:</strong> Products purchased, order amount, date of purchase.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>We do <strong>not</strong> collect or store payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor.</p>

  <h2>How We Use Your Information</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To process and fulfill your orders</li>
    <li>To send you order confirmations and tracking updates</li>
    <li>To respond to customer service inquiries</li>
    <li>With your consent, to send occasional product updates or promotions (you can unsubscribe anytime)</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
  <p>We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Stripe</strong> — for payment processing (see their privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy)</li>
    <li><strong>Shipping carriers</strong> — for order delivery</li>
    <li>As required by law</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Data Retention</h2>
  <p>We retain order information for as long as necessary to fulfill orders and comply with legal obligations (typically 3-5 years for tax purposes). You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.</p>

  <h2>Your Rights</h2>
  <p>You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email <a href="mailto:support@nobleparc.com">support@nobleparc.com</a>.</p>
