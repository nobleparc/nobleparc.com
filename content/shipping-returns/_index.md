<section class="page-section container">
  <h2>Shipping</h2>
  <p><strong>Free US shipping</strong> on every order — no minimum, no exceptions.</p>
  <p><strong>Shipping area:</strong> We ship exclusively to the <strong>continental United States</strong> (48 contiguous states). We are unable to ship to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, or other US territories at this time.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Processing:</strong> 1-2 business days after placing your order.</li>
    <li><strong>Delivery:</strong> 3-7 business days via standard US carrier (UPS / USPS).</li>
    <li><strong>Tracking:</strong> You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>At this time we ship exclusively within the United States. International shipping is not yet available.</p>

  <h2>Returns</h2>
  <p>We stand behind every product. If you're not satisfied for any reason, you may return it within <strong>30 days of delivery</strong> for a full refund.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Items must be in good condition (light wear from normal use is fine).</li>
    <li>Contact us at <a href="mailto:support@nobleparc.com">support@nobleparc.com</a> with your order number.</li>
    <li>We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label.</li>
    <li>Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive the return.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Packaging &amp; Privacy</h2>
  <p>All orders are shipped in <strong>discreet, neutral packaging</strong> with no branding, logos, or product descriptions on the outside. No invoices or promotional materials are included inside the package. Your privacy matters.</p>

  <h2>Warranty</h2>
  <p>Every Nobleparc device comes with a <strong>2-year limited warranty</strong> against manufacturing defects. If your device stops working due to a manufacturing issue, we'll replace it free of charge. This does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage, or water damage from misuse.</p>

  <h2>Questions?</h2>
  <p>Email <a href="mailto:support@nobleparc.com">support@nobleparc.com</a> — we typically respond within 24 hours.</p>
